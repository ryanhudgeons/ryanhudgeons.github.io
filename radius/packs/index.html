Street packs

The streets for the Radius minimap, one region at a time.

SupportPrivacyAll apps

Why a download instead of a live map? A live map asks a server for the streets around you every few seconds — which tells that server where you are. Radius never does that. You download a whole region once; that tells this site only which region you picked, never where you are in it. Radius itself never connects to anything to get streets.

How to add one

  1. Tap Download next to your region below.
  2. iPhone / iPad: when it finishes, open the .radius file from Safari's downloads or the Files app and choose Radius — or in Radius, Settings › Streets › Import a pack file.
  3. Android: tap the finished download and open it with Radius — or Settings › Streets › Import a pack file.

Up to six packs at once (2 GB total). Road trip? Add the regions you'll drive through and remove them afterwards.

What's in a pack

Roads and their names only — highways down to alleys — so the minimap can draw the streets and say which one you're on. No buildings, no addresses, no businesses, no house numbers. Each pack is cut from OpenStreetMap and refreshed about once a month; re-download to update (your zones stay as they are).
