Updated November 10, 2026

The safest data is data we never have. None of the apps listed on this site collects your data today. Your jobs, zones, scores and settings stay on your device.

How the apps are built

  • On the device. Everything you enter is stored only in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, so there is no password of yours for anyone to steal.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. No app here includes analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code.
  • No network code of their own. CCTV Calc, Cable Ledger, Radius and Little Pilots make no network connections of their own. Anything that leaves your device does so only because you started it, such as sharing a report, looking up an address or sending a support email. Each app's privacy policy lists exactly what.
  • Purchases through the store. Purchases and tips go through Apple, Google Play or, for Radius tips on Android, Ko-fi in your browser. We never see your card or learn who you are.

This website

The apps have no analytics. This website does: ljna2714.com uses HeyCatch analytics to count page views and clicks, so I can see which pages help people find the right tool. It doesn't ask for your name or email, it doesn't run ads, and nothing from the website is connected to the apps. Paw Pairs' pages and the kids' app pages don't load it.

Kids' apps

The kids' apps never collect a child's data or any personal information. They have no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

If that ever changes

Some future apps, such as business and community apps with sign-in, may need to collect a little, for example a phone number to sign in. That happens only with your agreement and security best practices in place, and we will say plainly what is collected and why, in the app, on its privacy page and in its store listing, before it ships.
