Built by someone who's run the jobs.

LJNA 2714 is an independent software studio, currently being organized as a limited liability company (formation in progress). Its founder spent years in the field — installing, servicing and running projects and operations across access control, security cameras, fire alarm, intercoms, data and telecom, on industrial, data-center and government sites.

Every app starts from a real jobsite problem: the math you do on a ladder, the paperwork after the pull, the question a crew asks twice a day. If you install for a living and something wastes your time, we want to hear about it.

Our promise on data

The free kids' apps are personal. They are being built for the founder's own children, so kids can learn and grow without scams, ads or data collection. LJNA 2714 keeps a strict policy on data, and it works the same way for every app.

Kids' apps & Radius

No data. Ever.

Kids' apps are free, with no ads, no accounts and no purchases, and they never collect a child's data or any personal information. Radius never collects data either. That will not change.

Professional tools

On your device today

Our tools for installers and crews, like CCTV Calc and Cable Ledger, keep job details on your device today. No accounts, no tracking.

Roadmap

Only with your agreement

Today, none of our published apps collect data. Some apps that are coming, such as business and community apps with sign-in and tools for specialized industries, will collect only what they need to work, for example a phone number to sign in. That happens only with your agreement and clear checks and balances in place, and we will say plainly what is collected and why, in the app, on its privacy page and in its store listing, before it starts.
