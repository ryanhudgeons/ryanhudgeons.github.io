Built by someone who's run the jobs.

LJNA 2714 is an independent software studio. Its founder spent years in the field — installing, servicing and running projects and operations across access control, security cameras, fire alarm, intercoms, data and telecom, on industrial, data-center and government sites.

Every app starts from a real jobsite problem: the math you do on a ladder, the paperwork after the pull, the question a crew asks twice a day. If you install for a living and something wastes your time, we want to hear about it.
