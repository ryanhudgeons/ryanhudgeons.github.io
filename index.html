Built for my kids first

Free kids' apps. No scams, no ads, no data.

These apps started as one app for my daughter and turned into tech. Now my daughters want to learn how it works, and one of them wants to be a pilot. That is why I build for kids first.

Every kids' app is educational and based on the real world. The math and the technology inside are the same ideas I have worked with in the field, simplified to a kid's level so the lessons land.

I'm building the kids' apps so children can learn and grow without scams, ads or data collection. They are free, they never collect a child's data or any personal information, and that will not change. Only paid apps and tools for specialized industries will ever have access to data, and only with your agreement.

Built by someone who's run the jobs.

LJNA 2714 is an independent software studio, currently being organized as a limited liability company (formation in progress). Its founder spent years in the field, installing, servicing and running projects and operations across access control, security cameras, fire alarm, intercoms, data and telecom, on industrial, data-center and government sites.

The name comes from my kids. The brand was born in New Mexico and grew up in Arizona, and the mark carries both: a saguaro on the chip for Arizona, and circuit traces that form the Zia sun for New Mexico. Our long-term plan is to design hardware here in the Southwest.

Every app starts from a real jobsite problem: the math you do on a ladder, the paperwork after the pull, the question a crew asks twice a day. If you install for a living and something wastes your time, I want to hear about it.

Our promise on data

LJNA 2714 keeps a strict data policy, and it works the same way for every app. Each app's privacy page says exactly what it collects, and its store listing says the same. How the apps handle data →

Kids' apps & Radius

No data. Ever.

The kids' apps are free, with no ads and no purchases, and they never collect a child's data or any personal information. Radius never collects data either. That will not change.

Professional tools

Nothing collected today

CCTV Calc and Cable Ledger keep job details on your device today, and their privacy pages say so. If that ever changes, it changes only as described in the roadmap card.

Roadmap

Only with your agreement

Some apps that are coming, such as business and community apps with sign-in and tools for specialized industries, will collect only what they need to work, for example a phone number to sign in. That happens only with your agreement and security best practices in place, and we will say plainly what is collected and why, in the app, on its privacy page and in its store listing, before it starts.
