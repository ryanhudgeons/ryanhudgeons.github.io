Prices outside the US

The App Store and Google Play set the price in your country's currency, with local taxes where they apply. The amount you see in the store is the amount you pay.

Refunds

Purchases go through Apple or Google, so refunds do too, under their policies: on iPhone and iPad through Apple's Report a Problem page, and on Android through your Google Play order history. If something in the app isn't working, email ryan@ljna2714.com and I'll help first.
