CCTV Calc — privacy policy

HomeSupportHow the Math WorksAll Apps

Effective November 22, 2026 (adds Android and website analytics)

CCTV Calc collects no data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. On Android it doesn't even ask for internet access. The only outside exchange is the store's own: Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, Google Play on Android. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Purchases and the free trial

On iPhone and iPad, the free trial and CCTV Calc Pro are in-app purchases handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's terms and Apple's privacy policy. The app asks Apple's StoreKit only whether this Apple Account has started the trial or bought Pro, and which version of the app was first downloaded (anyone who bought the app before it was free to download keeps Pro). Those answers stay on your device. The developer receives no personal information from a purchase, and the app sends no receipts or purchase details anywhere.

On Android, CCTV Calc Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play Billing, under Google's terms and Google's privacy policy. The app asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns Pro, and keeps just that yes or no on the phone. The free trial is the app's own record: its start date is kept on the phone. The app sends no purchase details anywhere.

What stays on your device

The jobs you save (camera groups, devices, lenses, notes), your own cameras and your settings (units, appearance, theme, language) are stored only on the device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them from the device. On iPhone and iPad, if you use iCloud device backup, Apple backs them up with the rest of your device under Apple's terms; the app itself does not use iCloud.

On Android, if backup is on, the phone's own backup to your Google account includes your saved jobs and settings, so a copy can stay in that backup after you delete the app and comes back if you reinstall it. Google handles that backup; the app doesn't use any cloud service of its own. Whether you own Pro is not backed up: Google Play is asked again on the restored phone.

Sharing

Reports you share as text, PDF or CSV go only when you tap Share, through your device's share sheet (iOS or Android) to the app you choose. That app's privacy policy covers what it does with them.

Ratings

On iPhone and iPad, the app may ask, through Apple's standard prompt, whether you would like to rate it. That prompt is Apple's; the app receives nothing from it. The Android app has no rating prompt.

This website

This website uses website analytics. The CCTV Calc app does not. It has no analytics or tracking code, so nothing recorded by this website is connected to the app or to anything in it.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.
