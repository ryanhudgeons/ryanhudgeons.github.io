Help test CCTV Calc, Radius and Cable Ledger on Android

All three apps are on the App Store now. Google will not let a new developer publish an app on Google Play until 12 people have tested it for 14 days. I need 12 testers for each app. Joining takes two taps.

Step 1. Join the tester group

Join the tester group

Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone. Members are not shown to each other, and nothing is emailed to you unless you choose.

Step 2. Pick your app

Test CCTV Calc

Camera job math in one app: NVR storage, PoE budget, UPS runtime, lens coverage and fire alarm battery. Works with no signal. No ads.

Test Radius

Set a line on the map and Radius warns you before you cross it, even with the screen off. Keep-in and keep-out zones for the jobsite. Free, no account, no tracking.

Outside the USA? Email ryan@ljna2714.com to request a map pack for your area or region.

Test Cable Ledger

Labels and port maps for low-voltage and structured-cabling crews. Every drop gets a number, a label and a port, tracked from pull to pass. Works offline. No account, no ads.

On that page tap "Become a tester", then install from Google Play.

Step 3. Keep it for 14 days

Stay in the test and keep the app installed for 14 days. Use it on a real job if you can.

What you get

CCTV Calc Pro free at Android launch, if you do two things:

  1. Stay in the test for the full 14 days.
  2. Email me one piece of real feedback at ryan@ljna2714.com, from the address you joined with.

Real feedback is one specific thing: a bug, a number that looked wrong, something missing, or the job you used it on.

To make it easy, answer any of these: What job did you try it on? What was confusing or wrong? What is missing?

Include your phone model.

I use your email only to send your code.

Cable Ledger Pro free when it arrives, for the same two things. Cable Ledger is free right now with nothing locked. Pro comes in a later update. Stay in the Cable Ledger test for the full 14 days and email me one piece of real feedback, and Pro is yours free on Android.

Radius is free for everyone.

Found a problem?

Email ryan@ljna2714.com.

If Google Play says the app is not available

Check that you joined the group with the same Google account your phone uses, then open the step 2 link again.
