CCTV Calc — privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026

SupportAll appsHow the math works

CCTV Calc collects no data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network connections. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

The jobs you save (camera groups, devices, lenses, notes) and your settings (units, appearance, language) are stored only on the device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple backs them up with the rest of your device under Apple's terms; the app itself does not use iCloud.

Sharing

When you share a report as text, PDF or CSV, iOS's share sheet hands the file to the app you choose. What that app does with it is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Ratings

The app may ask, through Apple's standard prompt, whether you would like to rate it. That prompt is Apple's; the app receives nothing from it.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: ljna2714@proton.me.
