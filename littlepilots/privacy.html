Little Pilots — privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

All apps

Little Pilots collects no data. No personal information is collected, stored on any server, or shared. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own.

What stays on the device

Stickers earned, the child's name and chosen pilot, and the game's settings are saved only in the app's own storage on the device. They are never sent anywhere, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Voice and sound

Captain Pip's voice is made on the device by the device's own text-to-speech voice. The app does not use the microphone, camera, photos, contacts or location.

The grown-ups guide and its links

Behind a press-and-hold Grown-ups button, the game has a guide for parents with the sources behind the lessons. Those sources are links to public websites (the FAA, the eCFR, AOPA and similar). Opening one leaves the app and opens that website in the device's browser, under that website's own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing when you do.

Children's privacy

Little Pilots is made for young children. Because the app collects no data at all, no information about a child is gathered, stored on a server, or transmitted. This is in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category.
